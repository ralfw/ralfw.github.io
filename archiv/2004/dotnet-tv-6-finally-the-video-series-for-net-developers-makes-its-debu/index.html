dotnet.tv 6 - Finally the Video Series for .NET Developers Makes its Debut in English

1. Mai 2004Erstveröffentlichung: weblogs.asp.net/ralfwOriginaladresseralfw.de/archiv/2004/dotnet-tv-6-finally-the-video-series-for-net-developers-makes-its-debu/

Finally we made it: After 5 installments of the German video series for .NET developers we did the current one in English. The topic WebServices called for a standard language ;-) and we wanted to see, how an international developer audience reacts to our efforts to explain .NET technologies using video.

As usual, I´ve written a "Making of..." diary of the production process. Take a look behind the scenes - and don´t be surprised if there are no guys doing a talk show or drawing on whiteboards. dotnet.tv is different from other video productions for developers! :-)

"Making of..." diaries (although in German) for previous dotnet.tv episodes can also be found in my blog.
